About PerfumeApp

PerfumeApp is a perfume recommendation and review application created for my Web Programming 2 project.

Users can browse perfumes, view perfume details and ratings, and logged-in users can add, edit and delete perfume information.

The application was created because I am very interested in perfumes and wanted to build an application around something I enjoy.Because ever since i was a kid i have a very sensitive nose and i can smell things that other people can't smell. I have a very good sense of smell and i can tell the difference between different perfumes.I have over 20 perfumes in my collection and i love to reccomend and try new recommendations.
